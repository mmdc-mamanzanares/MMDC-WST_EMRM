ABOUT

The Employee Maintenance and Records Management System (EMRMS) is a browser-based application designed to manage employee information through a centralized and organized platform.

Purpose

This system enables Human Resources personnel to maintain employee records efficiently, improve data accessibility, and support day-to-day employee management activities.

Target Users

  • Human Resources Staff
  • HR Managers
  • Department Managers
  • System Administrators
